ARTICLE III

DEVELOPER’S DUTIES

 

The Developer shall design, permit, and construct the offsite waterline in the location described in Article II(4)(c)(ii) and as shown on the plans, according to engineering best practices, requirements of the Town, and requirements of the North Carolina Department of Environment and Natural Resources (NCDENR) and any other regulatory agency. The Developer shall secure and dedicate to the Town all necessary easements for the construction and perpetual maintenance of the infrastructure referenced in Article 2, Sections 4(c)& (d).

 

The Developer shall construct in a workmanlike manner the Offsite and Onsite Public Infrastructure according to the design specifications and criteria of the Town which are existing at the time of this agreement, as well as full compliance with any NCDENR requirements. Said infrastructure, together with any and all appurtenances, easements, and equipment associated with the project required for public dedication shall be dedicated to the Town in a manner consistent with the development plan and in a manner in accordance with the Town’s standards or within the earlier of either 18 months after of the date Developer acquires title to the Applicable Parcels or 21 months after the date of execution of this document.

 

The Developer shall construct the public infrastructure according to the plans and specifications submitted in a manner consistent with the standards of the Town which are in effect at the time of this Agreement. Said construction of all offsite public infrastructure shall be completed no later than the earlier of either 18 months after the Developer acquires title to the Applicable Parcels or 21 months after the date of execution of this Agreement.

 

The Developer shall be responsible for the complete design and construction of the public infrastructure (as referenced in Article 2, Sections 4(c)& (d)), including all surveying, design, permitting, plat preparation and recordation, testing, engineering, bidding, construction, inspection, acceptance, easement maps and documents, construction management, construction administration, and all other necessary functions to ensure complete-in-place, fully functional infrastructure built and operational in accordance with Town and State standards and approved plans for the project in effect at the time of this Agreement. The costs of all of these items shall be borne by the Developer and shall not be subject to reimbursement or participation by the Town, except as set forth herein. The Town shall independently inspect the project and offsite infrastructure installation and all work shall be subject to the Town’s approval.

 

The Developer shall pay all development fees for the development at the time they would otherwise ordinarily be due and in an amount that is determined by the fee schedule approved in the annual budget which are in effect at the time of this Agreement. By way of example, a list of applicable development fees are attached hereto as Exhibit E.

 

 

 

ARTICLE IV

GENERAL TERMS

 

All reimbursements, if any, for this project shall be provided on the basis of final documented actual costs, as approved by the Director of Engineering, in accordance with this agreement. Any opinions of project cost included in this agreement are based upon preliminary designs.

 

The Developer shall be responsible for the complete design and construction of each project, including all surveying, design, permitting, plat preparation and recordation, testing, engineering, bidding, construction, inspection, acceptance, easement maps and documents, construction management, construction administration, and all other necessary functions to insure complete-in-place, fully functional projects built and operational in accordance with Town and State standards and approved plans for the project. The costs of all of these items shall be borne by the Developer and shall not be subject to reimbursement or participation by the Town, except as specifically described herein.

 

All fees, charges and payments related to the actual construction of each project shall be borne by the Developer and shall not be subject to reimbursement or participation by the Town, except as set forth herein.

 

In the course of installing the sewer line or any other work on easements obtained by the Town and under the Town’s ownership, the Developer shall operate under permission to encroach onto a Town easement and shall hold the Town harmless. The Developer agrees to protect, defend, indemnify and hold the {{owner_company_name}} and its officers, employees and agents free and harmless from and against any and all losses, penalties, damages, settlements, costs, charges, professional fees, or other expenses or liabilities of every kind in character and arising out of or relating to any and all claims, liens, demands, obligations, actions, proceedings, or causes of actions of every kind and character in connection with or arising directly out of the entry onto and performance of work on the easements of the Town. The Developer further agrees to investigate, handle, provide defense for and respond to, any such claims at its sole expense and agrees to bear all other costs and expenses related thereto, even if such claims are groundless, false, or fraudulent.

 

All projects shall be subject to the Town’s policies and procedures regarding Town acceptance and warranty on infrastructure in effect at the time of this Agreement.

 

It shall be the responsibility of the Developer to ensure that all regulatory approvals for each project are in place before beginning construction.

 

Any Town-funded activities must follow public bidding laws regardless of whether or not the Town or the Developer is the contracting entity. For each project that the developer is constructing that is reimbursable under this Agreement, the Developer shall provide proof of compliance with all applicable provisions of the North Carolina General Statues regulating public contracts. Primarily, this involves North Carolina General Statues 143-129 “Formal Bidding Procedure” or NCGS 143-131 “Informal Bidding Procedure” whichever shall apply based upon the total cost of the project constructed as part of this Agreement. The bid shall include unit prices. The Developer, or his designee, shall provide copies of all bid proposals received, a copy of the executed contract between the Developer and the lowest, responsible bidder, and a unit price bid tabulation which is signed and sealed by a professional engineer registered in the State of North Carolina certifying the bids received and the award of the contract are in conformance with the aforementioned provisions. Should the Developer award the project to a bidder other than the lowest bidder, all reimbursements from the Town and in accordance with the terms of the Agreement shall be based upon the lowest bid. Furthermore, if a bidder other than the low bidder is awarded the contract, then in that case, the Town would not participate in any change order which would increase the overall cost of the project and the reimbursement would be based upon the original low bid.

 

 

 

ARTICLE V

TOWN’S DUTIES

 

1. Town shall, upon a proper request for vested rights by the Developer, consider the request pursuant to N.C.G.S. §160A-385.1, et. seq. and shall use its sole legislative discretion in its decision without influence of any covenant contained herein.

 

2. Town shall construct the off-site sewer extension as shown on Exhibit B, as referenced in Article II(4)(a), above. The Town has currently in place, or has the legal authority to subsequently acquire within the period of time called for in this agreement, all necessary easements and rights of way to provide sanitary sewer service to the Applicable Parcels.

 

3. Town shall reimburse the Developer for actual cost spent on the difference, as calculated by a sealed engineer’s estimate, between installation of the 12-inch waterline along RS Road South from the existing bore along NC 55, as described in Article II, Section 4(c)(ii)(2), above, by reimbursing fee credits against water capacity replacement fees and water acreage fees in an amount not to exceed that amount which would be due and owing by the Developer for the Development.
